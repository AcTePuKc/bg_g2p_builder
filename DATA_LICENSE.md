## Practical guidance

- Do not assume that Apache-2.0 alone governs generated lexicon files.
- Review the exact license terms of each upstream dataset before redistributing
  generated lexical outputs.
- When in doubt, preserve attribution, keep provenance records, and disclose
  that generated outputs may contain Wiktionary-derived material.
